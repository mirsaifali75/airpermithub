General Air Quality Permits 
At this time, qualifying applicants may receive general air quality permits for the following source categories: 
Title V Air Curtain Incinerators
Non-Title V: Emergency Generators
Cotton Ginning
Yarn Spinning Plants
Concrete Batch Plants
NOTE : If you apply for and receive a general permit, you will be subject to enforcement action for operating without a permit if your facility or source is later determined not to qualify for the terms and conditions of the general permit.
Details on each general permit type and qualifications is provided below. 
Title V General Permits
Under 15A NCAC 02Q .0509, DAQ may issue a permit to cover numerous similar facilities or sources provided the following conditions are met:
There is no difference between the facilities or sources that would require special permit conditions for any individual facility or source; AND
No unique analysis is required for any facility or source covered by the permit.
General permits issued under 02Q .0509 shall identify criteria by which facilities or sources may qualify for the permit. To facilities or sources that qualify, the Director shall grant the terms and conditions of the permit.
Sources subject to Title IV shall not be eligible for a permit issued pursuant to this Rule.
If your facility or source qualifies for a general permit issued pursuant to 02Q .0509, you shall apply for coverage by the terms of the permit issued pursuant to 02Q .0509 or shall apply for a standard permit for each facility or source pursuant to 02Q .0500.
General permits issued pursuant to 02Q .0509 are not required to undergo the public participation procedures pursuant to 15A NCAC 02Q .0521.
Air Curtain Incinerators
General Title V air quality permits may be issued for Commercial and Industrial Solid Waste (CISWI) Air Curtain Incinerator (ACI) units subject to NSPS Subpart CCCC burning more than 35 tons per day of only the following:
100% wood waste;
100% clean lumber, and
100% mixture of only wood waste, clean lumber, and/or yard waste
ACI general Title V air quality permit applications are now available to submit online through AccessDEQ .

What Activities Require This Permit?
Air curtain incinerators (ACIs) that burn only 100 percent wood waste, 100 percent clean lumber, and/or 100 percent mixture of wood waste, clean lumber and/or yard waste. Air curtain incinerators that burn only less than 35 tons per day of 100 percent wood waste, 100 percent clean lumber, 100 percent yard waste or 100 percent mixture of only the materials listed above collected from the general public and from residential, commercial, institutional, and industrial sources; or air curtain incinerators located at institutional facilities that burn only 100 percent wood waste, 100 percent clean lumber, 100 percent yard waste or 100 percent mixture of only the materials listed above generated at that facility. Facilities eligible for General Title V Air permits must meet certain conditions and emissions must be below thresholds established by rule.
What Is the Purpose of This Permit?
To reduce adverse effects of industrial and other activities on air quality and to protect the health of the citizens of North Carolina.
Who Issues This Permit?
N.C. Division of Air Quality (DAQ), Title V Permitting Section, Raleigh Central Office.
How Much Will This Permit Cost?
Fees for General Title V Air Permits for ACIs burning only the clean biomass listed above are 10 percent of the otherwise applicable fee.
What Are My Payment Options for Permit Application Fees?
Currently the DAQ accepts checks as well as ePayments for payment of the Permit Application Fees.
If Paying by Check, Whom do I Make the Check Payable to and Where Do I Send the Check? 
Permit Application Fees are to be submitted with the completed application. The application will be considered incomplete for processing until the fee is received. The check should be made payable to DEQ Division of Air Quality. Please note: The Raleigh Central Office Permitting Section (address provided below) receives permit applications for ACIs.
Attn: Mark Cuilla, Chief, Permitting Section, Division of Air Quality, 1641 Mail Service Center Raleigh, North Carolina 27699-1641
How Long Is My Permit or License or Certificate Valid?
General Title V Air Permits are issued for five years.
How Long Will It Take to Review My Application?
The director shall issue or deny the permit within 90 days of receipt of a complete application or 10 days after receipt of requested additional information, whichever is later.
Where Do I Submit My Application?
DAQ Regional Office appropriate for your location. Regional Offices | NC DEQ 
Non-Title V General Permits
Under 15A NCAC 02Q .0310, DAQ may issue a permit to cover numerous similar facilities or sources provided the following conditions are met:
There is no unique difference that would require special permit conditions for any individual facility; AND
No unique analysis is required for any facility covered under the permit.
General Permits issued under 02Q .0310 shall identify criteria by which facilities or sources may qualify for the permit. The Director shall grant the terms and conditions of the permit to facilities or sources that qualify.
