Hot Mix Asphalt General Permit
This general permit may cover all new and existing hot mix asphalt producing facilities in the State of Mississippi which fall under the Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) Code 2951 – manufacturing asphalt paving mixtures and block (hot-mixed asphalt). The Hot Mix Asphalt General Permit was reissued on October 24, 2022 and modified on September 27, 2023 . Forms pertaining to coverage, recoverage, modification or termination may be submitted through MDEQ’s online portal here . However, a hard copy with signature must also be submitted to:
