AccessDEQ
Bringing DEQ’s online tools together
AccessDEQ is your digital hub to easily access online tools in one convenient place. Created to increase public access and streamline the department’s permitting processes, AccessDEQ provides convenient public access to:
Permit Applications: Select permit applications are now available online. As AccessDEQ continues to expand, more permits across our regulatory divisions will be available for online applications.
Online Public Records: AccessDEQ provides instant public access to many DEQ documents and files.
Maps & Data: Interactive maps and data tools, including environmental sampling data from key divisions are accessible in one place.
CROMERR Compliance
NCDEQ is required by federal law to comply with the Cross-Media Electronic Reporting Rule (CROMERR), established by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). This rule supports the secure submission of electronic documents by providing standards for identity proofing. To learn more, visit: Learn about the Cross-Media Electronic Reporting Rule (CROMERR) | US EPA
