Modifying or Applying for an Air Quality Permit 
Application Forms for Air Quality Permit to Construct/Operate Non-Title V and Title V Facilities 
Use our Application Matrix to determine which application forms are required.
A $25.00 processing fee will be charged for returned checks in accordance with the North Carolina General Statue 25-3-512 
You must submit the following number of permit applications and supporting data to DAQ: 
One copy of the permit application for any facility (whether Title V or non-Title V).
Two copies of any modeling analysis/report and one copy of the modeling files. 
Mail the application and all documents to the appropriate NC DAQ Regional Office . Alternatively, if applying for a Title V air quality permit, you may upload your application using the AccessDEQ portal . See our User Guide for more information about AccessDEQ.
