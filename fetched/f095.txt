Modifying or Applying for an Air Quality Permit 
Air Quality Application Tool
Facilities can now submit Title V air quality permit application electronically via AccessDEQ instead of mailing applications to our regional offices. We plan to expand this tool to non-Title V permit applications later this year.
AccessDEQ User Guide 
Application Forms & Information
Permit Application Forms : Permit application forms for Title V and non-Title V Facilities
Small Facilities : Permit application, permit exemption, and registration for Small facilities
Title V Minor Modifications : Implementing Title V minor modifications prior to permit revision in accordance with 15A NCAC 02Q .0515
502(b)(10) Changes : Notification form for Title V 502(b)(10) changes and application forms
General Air Quality Permits : General permits are available for the following source types:
Title V Sources (in accordance with 15A NCAC 02Q .0509): New Air Curtain Incinerator units burning more than 35 tons per day of only 100% wood waste, 100% clean lumber, and 100% mixture of wood waste, clean lumber and/or yard waste.
Non-Title V Sources (in accordance with 15A NCAC 02Q .0310): Emergency Generators, Cotton Ginning, Yarn Spinning Plants, and Concrete Batch Plants.
Zoning Consistency Determination : All air quality permit applications must include a zoning consistency determination request in order to be deemed complete. NOTE: Public notice is required for air permits in areas without zoning.
