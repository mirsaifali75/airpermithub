Commercial and Special Waste Haulers

Please complete the following form, which serves as your application. Write Not Applicable (NA) if a question does not apply to your operation. Most questions require only a short answer. A short phrase or one or two sentences may be enough to answer the question fully.
The information on this application must describe your actual operations. When operations change, please resubmit a revised version of this form within 30 days. If actual operations differ from that described in this application and operations plan, the commercial hauler may be issued a violation.

If necessary, further explanation for any question may be given in the space at the end of the form. Fees are $100 for haulers with two trucks or fewer and no special waste, and $300 for haulers with three or more trucks or any special waste. Fees must be paid by check before the application is processed. Haulers do not need separate registration for collection/storage facilities if they operate below 240 cubic yards per day, do not serve the public, and include the facility’s operations in their hauler registration.
