Interested Party Review — OAC rule 3745-3103 — Permit Exemptions
What does OAC rule 3745-31-03 cover?
The rules in Ohio Administrative Code (OAC) Chapter 3745-31 require all new and modified sources of air pollution apply for and
obtain a permit-to-install (PTI) or a permit-to-install and operate (PTIO) before beginning installation and commencing
operation. These permits identify applicable air pollution control rules and regulations under which the source must operate
and establish monitoring, record keeping, testing and reporting requirements by which the sources demonstrate compliance
with the rules and regulations. OAC rule 3745-31-03 contains exemptions from the requirement to obtain a PTI or PTIO.

What step is this in the rulemaking process?
This step in Ohio EPA’s rulemaking process, required by Ohio Revised Code (ORC) 121.39, is called Interested Party Review.
During this step, the Agency makes available draft rules for interested parties for review and comment. This step occurs after
the Early Stakeholder Outreach comment period.

What changes are being considered?
During the previous review of OAC Chapter 3745-31, Ohio EPA created several new permit exemptions. While preparing the U.S.
EPA submittal package for these rules to become part of the State Implementation Plan, some clarifications were identified that
required revisions to the new permanent exemptions for sand and gravel storage and handling operations, animal crematories,
portable sawmills and temporary portable flares.

Who will be regulated by these rules?
Only facilities listed in the exemptions qualify for the exemption from the requirements to obtain a PTI or PTIO.

What is the rulemaking schedule?
Upon completion of the Interested Party Review portion of this rulemaking, Ohio EPA will make any changes necessary to the
rule language. After considering comments, the rules will be filed with JCARR (Joint Committee on Agency Rule Review).
