New York State Department of Environmental Conservation
Annual Capping Certification
As required by 6 NYCRR Part 201-7.1(h), facilities that have been issued a federally enforceable emissions cap
must certify compliance with that cap on an annual basis. The Department has developed the attached form
which may be used to comply with this requirement. Completed forms must be submitted to the Regional Air
Pollution Control Engineer for the DEC Region in which the facility is located. A list of DEC regional offices and
their contact information is available on the Department’s website at:
http://www.dec.ny.gov/about/76070.html. Please complete each section of the form as follows:
I. Certification of Truth, Accuracy, and Completeness
Facility Information:
DEC ID: Enter the facility’s DEC ID (found on the facility’s air permit).
Report Type: Enter the report type.
Reporting Period: Enter the beginning and ending dates for the reporting period covered by this form.
Facility Name: Enter the facility name as printed on the air permit. Do not use a colloquial or
abbreviated name.
Mailing Address: Enter the complete mailing address for the facility.
Facility Contact: Enter the name, telephone number, and e-mail address for the facility contact person. The
contact person entered in this section should be the person most familiar with the day-to-day operation of the
facility and the details included in the submission (e.g. the plant manager) that is available to be contacted by
the Department if necessary.
Responsible Official: Enter the name, mailing address, telephone number, and e-mail address of the
responsible official. A responsible official is defined in Paragraph 201-2.1(b)(28). The responsible official listed
in this section must also sign and date the certification line. By signing the certification line, the responsible
official is certifying that the information contained on the form and any attachments is true, accurate, and
complete. Note: The responsible official must also sign and date each capping certification form submitted.
II. Annual Capping Certification Form
DEC ID: Enter the facility’s DEC ID (found on the facility’s air permit).
Facility Name: Enter the facility name as printed on the air permit. Do not use a colloquial or
abbreviated name.
Reporting Period: Enter the beginning and ending dates for the reporting period covered by this form.
Capped Regulation: Enter the regulation for which the facility has accepted an emissions cap. The
regulation should be entered as stated in the facility’s permit.

Contaminant Name: Enter the name of the contaminant that is being capped. A separate form must
be filled out for each individual contaminant capped at the facility.
CAS No.: Enter the Chemical Abstract Series (CAS) number for the capped contaminant. A separate
form must be filled out for each individual contaminant capped at the facility.
Capping Parameter/Limit: Enter the capping parameter and limit as stated in the permit. Include the
units of measurement applicable to the limit (i.e. NOx emissions / 24 tons per year; Residual oil burned
/ 1 million gallons per year; etc.).
Permit Level: Enter the permit level where the cap applies (i.e. Facility, Emission Unit, Emission Source,
Emission Point, Process, or combination). For a level other than facility, indicate the ID numbers
involved as given in the permit.
Method Used to Determine Emissions: Enter the method used to determine compliance with the cap
(i.e. CEMS data, fuel analysis, coating VOC analysis etc.). For emission units where a CEMS is used to
measure emissions, a secondary method must be used to estimate emissions during periods when the
CEMS did not operate. Note: emissions from all periods of operation must be accounted for when
determining compliance with an emissions cap.
Emissions Data:
Month and Year: Enter the month and year for the emissions described in this row.
Monthly Total Emissions: Enter the monthly total emissions for the contaminant listed above during
the month and year indicated. Include any formulas and/or calculations that were used to verify
compliance with the emissions cap as an attachment to the capping certification form.
12 Month Rolling Total Emissions: Enter the 12 month rolling total for the 12-month period that
includes the listed Month and Year. The 12-month period is defined in Paragraph 201-2.1(b)(1). The
12 month rolling total is not required for emissions caps that involve a monthly limit rather than an
annual limit. Include any formulas and/or calculations that were used to verify compliance with the
emissions cap as an attachment to the capping certification form.
Note: For limits on a basis other than monthly or annual, contact the appropriate DEC Regional Office
for instructions on what format is acceptable for reporting. A list of DEC regional offices and their
contact information is available on the Department’s website at:
http://www.dec.ny.gov/about/76070.html.
The responsible official named in Section I of the form must also sign and date each capping form
submitted.
