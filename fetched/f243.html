Our Clean Air Tracking System (CATS) allows users to process new applications, renewals, amendments and all other air permit related actions.

Access CATS

New air permit applicants must register for an account. To get started:

  • Download the Steps to Register as a General User (Owner/Agent/Licensed Professional)
  • Download the Fee Schedule
  • Download the Download the Procedures for Submitting a Payment
  • Download the Procedures for Filing a Cancellation

Users can also conduct an Air Permit Information search for a property.

Boiler and Burner Compliance Certification Form

A Boiler and Burner Compliance Certification is submitted by the filling PE to certify equipment (bare boiler, burner, assemblies, custom installation and control) not accepted by DEP will comply with all applicable regulations.

  • Download the Boiler and Burner Compliance Certification Form

Stack Adequacy

Draft calculations are required for a new boiler, induced draft fan or chimney and in accordance with the procedures in 2009 ASHRAE Fundamentals Chapter 21 and 2012 ASHRAE Systems and Equipment Chapter 35.

  • Download the Stack Adequacy Form
  • Download the Stack Adequacy Form Instructions

Download the Annual Boiler Tune-up Guidelines

All boilers that are equal to or greater than 2.8 million Btu/hr must have annual tune-ups and records of the tune-up using these forms must be retained for a minimum of 5 years.

Industrial Processes and Generators

Filing Instructions and Forms for Industrial Processes and Generators

If you require require assistance in determining which application process you should follow, please download and submit an Industrial Process Questionnaire.

Industrial Registrations

  • Download the procedures for General Registration—Process Sources, Mobile Food Trucks and Char Boilers
  • Download the procedures for General Registration—Char Boilers and Solid Fuel Burning Equipment. Solid Fuel Registration is required in order for DEP to conduct a Smoke and Odor Inspection. Once an application is approved, DEP will reach out to schedule an inspection.
  • Download the procedures for Industrial Registration—Gas Station.
  • Download the procedures for Industrial Registration—Engines, Generators and Other Combustion Sources.
    • For filing requirements for engines and generators, download Engines, Generators, Turbines Determination of NYC DEP Filing Requirements. Units below the listed thresholds do not need to be filed.

Industrial Forms

  • Download the Opacity Certification Form for Professional Engineers
  • Method 9 Opacity Certification is required for all generators regardless of their EPA Tier. This form should be completed and attached with an Inspection Request for original and triennial renewals through CATS. First time filing of EPA Tier 4 or equivalent generators do not need an opacity certification. Generators permitted as a registration must conduct an opacity test but do not need to submit the results to DEP. Download the Method 9—Opacity Certification Form
