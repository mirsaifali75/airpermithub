Air General Permit
Coverage under the following general permits is available to eligible applicants through the DWEE’s online application process. General Construction Permits are denoted with a (GCP) and General Operating Permits with a (GOP). You should read and understand the conditions of the permit you are interested in before starting the application for coverage process.
Click on the permit document link above to view the general permit you are interested in.
Aggregate Processing Plant (GCP) 
Asphalt Plant – Drum Mix Hot Mix (GCP) 
Asphalt Plant – Batch Mix Hot Mix (GCP) 
Concrete Plant – Truck Mix (uncontrolled loadout) (GCP) 
Concrete Plant – Truck Mix (controlled loadout) (GCP) 
Emergency Engine 1 (up to 10,000 hp, 2014 or later, diesel) (GCP) 
Emergency Engine 2 (up to 5,000 hp, diesel) (GCP) 
Incinerator (controlled) (GCP) 
Incinerator Operating (GOP) 
Surface Coating (GCP) 
The link below takes you to the online registration and application login page. New Users need to register for an online account first in order to use the process.
Online Application Process
Once you have registered and obtained an account, you can login to the application process. Please review the following materials if this is your first time using the online application process:
16-010 DWEE’s Online Application for Air General Permits 
16-011 General Air Permits – Construction and Operating 
Important note: Coverage under any of these online air general permits is not available for proposed new locations in:
Lancaster County
City of Omaha
Tribal Lands
For locations within the jurisdictional boundaries of Lancaster County, City of Omaha, and tribal lands, please instead contact:
Lincoln-Lancaster County Health Department - Air Programs, 3131 "O" Street, Lincoln, Nebraska 68510, (402) 441-8021
Omaha Public Works Department - Air Quality Control Division, 5600 South 10th Street, Omaha, Nebraska 68107, (402) 444-6015
Air Permitting and Compliance Branch - U.S. EPA Region 7 , 11201 Renner Boulevard, Lenexa, KS 66219, (913) 551-7020, and, if appropriate, the Tribe for those Tribal Lands administered by a Tribe.
