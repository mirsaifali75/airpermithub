Clean Air Permitting in New Jersey 
EPA's Region 2 serves New Jersey, New York, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands and 8 Tribal Nations 
New Jersey
Clean Air Act permitting in New Jersey (minor New Source Review (NSR), non-attainment NSR, PSD, and Part 70 Operating permits) is the responsibility of the New Jersey Air Quality Permitting Program of the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection.
