Department Proposed Rulemaking 
The Maine Department of the Secretary of State, Bureau of Corporations, Elections and Commissions maintains online copies of agency rule chapters .
The electronic version maintained by the Department of the Secretary of State and are in MS Word format unless otherwise noted. Rule chapters downloaded from the site should not be considered "official" state rules for use before a court. Contact the Secretary of State's Administrative Procedure Act Office for certified copies of rule chapters.
For general questions regarding Department Rulemaking please contact Erle Townsend (207) 287-6115. For specific questions regarding Rules applicability please contact program staff .
