Applications and Forms
Applications and forms are grouped by permit type and are available for download in either DOC or PDF format. Some documents require following a link to another page which may be served by an outside website.
You can submit electronic application for individual permits here and electronic applications for general permit coverages here . 
Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) is currently assessing the impact of the US Supreme Court ruling in the case of Sackett v. Environmental Protection Agency on the permitting process in Mississippi. During this period, it should be assumed that any wastewater discharge that was previously permitted will still necessitate obtaining permits through MDEQ. If you have any inquiries regarding this matter, please reach out to the Environmental Permitting Division.

Sewer Plans & Specs
The Environmental Permits Division (EPD) will accept and encourages the submission of Plans and Specifications in an electronic file formats such as PDF, TIFF on CD, or DVD. In accordance with state regulation 11 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. 6, R.1.1.6.B., EPD receives, reviews and approves plans and specifications for all proposed municipal and domestic sewage collections prior to beginning construction of proposed system. In the past, engineering firms have submitted full scale blueprint sets for these projects; however, this is no longer required in hardcopy format. At this time, the required information sheet should still be submitted via paper and a signed and stamped cover letter should be included with any electronic files indicating that the plans and specifications submitted electronically were developed by a professional engineer who holds a valid certificate of registration as a professional engineer issued by the Mississippi Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Surveyors .
