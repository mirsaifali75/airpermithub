List of Exempt Activities
Instructions
Applicants for Title V facility permits must provide a listing of each exempt activity, as described in 6 NYCRR Part 201-3.2(c), that is currently operated at the
facility. This form must be provided with each application for a new Title V facility permit and Title V facility permit renewal, or whenever changes are necessary.
In order to complete this form, enter the number and building location of each exempt activity conducted. Building IDs used on this form should match those
used in the Title V permit application. Provide all additional information where requested. If a listed activity is not operated at the facility, leave the
corresponding information blank.
