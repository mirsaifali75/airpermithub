Clean Air Act Permitting in North Carolina
Clean Air Act permitting in North Carolina is the shared responsibility of the North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality, the Forsyth County Department of Environmental Assistance and Protection, the Mecklenburg County Land Use and Environmental Services Agency, the Asheville-Buncombe Air Quality Agency and EPA Region 4. This page provides more information about the responsible agencies.
The EPA Region 4 Title V Permitting Contact for North Carolina is Estelle Bae - (404) 562-9143.
