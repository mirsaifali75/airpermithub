Air Quality Data
The RAPCA air monitoring program is required by the Clean Air Act and U.S EPA to determine compliance with the National Ambient Air Quality Standards ( NAAQS ). The network provides real time air quality data to the public on a daily basis. Quality-assured data is also uploaded each month to U.S. EPA’s Air Quality System (AQS) Data Mart. Please visit U.S. EPA’s Air Data website to access data collected by our agency. Should you have any questions or need help finding a particular data set, please contact us. 
Real time data can also be accessed on AirNow . 
The RAPCA area is currently designated by U.S. EPA in attainment for all NAAQS. If you are interested in learning more, visit Ohio EPA’s Attainment Designation page .

Pollen Information
RAPCA no longer collects daily pollen and mold counts. Historical counts are maintained on this website and can be requested. We recommend anyone interested in pollen and mold readings visit the American Academy of Allergy Asthma & Immunology and the National Allergy Bureau for certified accurate data, Pollen.com or visit their preferred weather app.

About Us
The Regional Air Pollution Control Agency (RAPCA) is the air pollution agency serving the citizens of Clark, Darke, Greene, Miami, Montgomery and Preble counties. RAPCA is a bureau of the Office of Environmental Health within Public Health - Dayton & Montgomery County.
