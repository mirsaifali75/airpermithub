Pacific Southwest Region (R9) 
Clean Air Act Permitting in Nevada
EPA’s Pacific Southwest (Region 9) implements and enforces federal environmental laws in Arizona, California, Hawaii, Nevada, the Pacific Islands, and 148 Tribal Nations. 
Clean Air Act permitting in Nevada is the shared responsibility of one state and two local agencies, along with EPA Region 9. More information about the responsible agencies is provided on this page.
EPA Region 9 is responsible for issuing permits on Tribal Lands in Nevada.
