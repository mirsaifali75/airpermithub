Clean Air Act Permitting in South Carolina
Clean Air Act permitting in South Carolina is the shared responsibility of the South Carolina Department of Environmental Services and EPA Region 4. More information about the responsible agencies is provided on this page.
The EPA Region 4 Title V Permitting Contacts for South Carolina is Kathleen Musmanno - (404) 562-9170.
