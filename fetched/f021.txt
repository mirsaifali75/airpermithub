Pre-Application Meetings for Air Quality Construction Permits
The Nebraska Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (DWEE) offers a pre-application
meeting for any applicant in need of an air quality construction permit. Pre-application meetings can be
beneficial for applicants in new or specialty industries, New Source Review (NSR) projects, modification
projects, or for any other project that may be complex in nature. The meeting can help establish open
communication between applicants and the DWEE, as well as help ensure that each permit application
is as complete and accurate as possible. This can help to minimize the time needed by DWEE to work
with the applicant to prepare a draft permit for public notice. In some cases, it may be helpful to
schedule a preliminary “project planning” meeting or conference call when the proposed project is in its
early planning stages and before setting up the pre-application meeting. To schedule a meeting for a
proposed project, please contact the Air Program by email at DWEE.AirQuality@nebraska.gov.
Timing
Consider requesting a pre-application meeting when you have a majority of your application completed,
typically one to three months prior to the planned permit application submittal. If you have worked
through a majority of the application before the meeting, you will be better prepared to discuss the
specifics of your planned application and provide information needed for the DWEE to ask and respond
to more specific and detailed questions.
Meeting Location
Pre-application meetings are held at the offices of DWEE, 245 Fallbrook Blvd., in Lincoln. If you would
like to have the pre-application meeting at an alternate location or the project site, please contact
DWEE to discuss options.
Meeting Participants
It is important that you provide the DWEE with an agenda well in advance of any meeting to help
ensure that the DWEE has time to review any materials and has the appropriate DWEE personnel in
attendance. Appropriate staff may include personnel from the Permitting and Engineering Division,
Inspection and Compliance Division, and possibly representatives from other state agencies or the US
EPA. Your representatives might include a Responsible Official for the owner/operator, a plant
manager, project engineer, or environmental consultant.
Information Needed Prior to Pre-application Meeting
Please provide an agenda to the DWEE at least one week prior to the planned meeting date. Consider
