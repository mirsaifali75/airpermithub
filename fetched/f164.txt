Clean Air Act Permitting in Nebraska
Clean Air Act permitting in Nebraska is the shared responsibility of the Nebraska Department of Environment and Energy, the Lincoln-Lancaster County Health Department, the Omaha Public Works Department and EPA Region 7. More information about the responsible agencies is available on this page.

Responsibilities
Issues permits state-wide except in Indian Country. Permits include construction permits for minor sources, major source PSD and nonattainment permitting and Title V operating permits
Issues permits in Indian Country, including Iowa Tribe, Omaha Tribe, Ponca Tribe, Sac & Fox Nation of Missouri, Santee Sioux Nation and Winnebago Tribe
