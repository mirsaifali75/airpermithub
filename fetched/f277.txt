Oil and Gas Well-site Production Operations with a Small Flare (GP 12.1)
These links are used to determine qualification for and/or applying for a model general permit (GP) for oil and gas well-site production operations where the flare is limited to a nominal 10 mmBtu/hr and the natural gas engines are limited to 1,800 total horsepower. This model GP has been developed to include all operations involved in an oil and gas well-site during the production phase of the well, including glycol dehydration units, natural gas and diesel engines, fixed roof storage tanks, flares and ancillary equipment.
*As of the effective date of 40 CFR Part 60 Subpart OOOOb, May 7, 2024, this GP will no longer be available for new sources. Renewal GPs will still be issued, as long as the sources are not affected facilities under 40 CFR Part 60 Subpart OOOOb. Ohio EPA is developing a new GP to incorporate these rule changes.

Oil and Gas Well-site Production Operations with a Small Flare (GP 12.3.1)
The Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, Division of Air Pollution Control (DAPC) is making model general permits (MGP) for oil and gas well site production operations, which commenced construction, modification or reconstruction after Dec. 6, 2022, available for application. The general permits incorporate 40 CFR Part 60 Subpart OOOOb requirements, and will authorize the installation of the following emission units at oil and gas well site production operations:
Fugitive Equipment/Pipeline Leaks
Dehydration System(s)
Spark Ignition Internal Combustion Engines
Enclosed or Open Flare(s)/Combustion Device(s)
Blowdowns/Rod Packing Emissions
Truck Loading
Condensate and/or Produced Water Tank Batteries
