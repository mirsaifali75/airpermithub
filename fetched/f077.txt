Clean Air Act Permitting in Missouri
Clean Air Act permitting in Missouri is the shared responsibility of the Missouri Department of Natural Resources (MoDNR) and EPA Region 7. More information about the responsible agencies is available on this page.

Responsibilities
Issues permits state-wide. Permits include construction permits and operating permits such as major source PSD and nonattainment permitting and Title V permits.
No federally recognized tribes in Missouri.
